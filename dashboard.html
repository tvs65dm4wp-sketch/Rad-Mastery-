High-value shortcuts

Tips & Tricks

Study methods, positioning reminders, physics mnemonics, clinical workflow tips, and registry strategy.

PHYSICS

Separate kVp from mAs

Think energy and penetration for kVp; photon quantity for mAs. Build the relationship first, then add exceptions and digital-imaging nuance.

POSITIONING

Evaluate before repeating

Before a repeat, identify whether the problem is rotation, centering, anatomy coverage, motion, exposure, or artifact. Correct the cause—not just the appearance.

REGISTRY

Review missed questions by concept

Do not memorize the answer choice. Write the concept that made the other options wrong and revisit it 24–48 hours later.
