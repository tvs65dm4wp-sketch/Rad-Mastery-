Refund Policy

This is a draft template and should be reviewed before publication.

Digital products

Because digital products can be accessed or downloaded immediately, purchases may generally be nonrefundable after access is provided, except where required by law or where the product is materially defective.

Technical issues

Customers should contact support if they cannot access purchased materials. Reasonable efforts should be made to restore access or provide replacement files.

Duplicate purchases

Verified duplicate transactions should be reviewed and refunded where appropriate.

Contact

Refund requests should be sent to support@yourdomain.com with the customer’s order number and purchase email.
