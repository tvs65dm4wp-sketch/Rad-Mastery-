Bremsstrahlung

A projectile electron passes near the positively charged tungsten nucleus. It changes direction and slows. The kinetic energy it loses is emitted as an X-ray photon. Variable energy loss creates a continuous spectrum.

Deep dive

Characteristic

A projectile electron ejects an inner-shell electron. A higher-shell electron drops into the vacancy. The binding-energy difference is released as a discrete X-ray photon characteristic of tungsten.
