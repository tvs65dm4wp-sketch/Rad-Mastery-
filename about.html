Our mission

Make difficult radiography concepts easier to understand.

Rad Mastery — The 1% is being built as an independent educational platform for students who want organized lessons, original practice questions, visual explanations, and a clearer way to track progress.

The idea

Created from the student perspective

The platform is intended to focus on concepts students commonly find difficult, including x-ray production, exposure relationships, digital imaging, radiation safety, patient care, and image evaluation.

Content should be carefully reviewed before publication and improved through feedback from students and qualified radiography educators.

Core principles

  • Original educational content
  • Clear and practical explanations
  • Respect for patient safety and professional standards
  • No copied examination questions
  • Continuous technical review and improvement
