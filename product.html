Product FAQ

Before purchasing

Will this guarantee that I pass an exam?

No educational product can guarantee an examination result. Success depends on formal education, clinical knowledge, preparation, and individual performance.

Are these real examination questions?

No. Questions should be original and written to reinforce radiography concepts without copying protected exam content.

How do students access the course?

The final version can use secure accounts and a database. This current launch candidate is still a front-end demonstration.

Can I share the files?

No. Purchased materials should be licensed for the individual customer’s personal educational use.
